Internal Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 | TetraSync Technologies Internal Systems

1. Overview

TetraSync Technologies is committed to safeguarding personal information collected during the account registration and authentication process within our internal employee workspace.

2. Data Collected

We collect and process the following categories of information for workplace access management:

  • Account Identifiers: Full legal name, unique username, encrypted password hashes (salted scrypt), and department/role assignments.
  • Activity Records: Task creation timestamps, progress update notes, status transition history, and administrative approval/rejection audit logs.
  • Session Information: HttpOnly cryptographic session cookies and last active login timestamps.

3. Purpose of Processing

Information is processed exclusively to maintain access security, authenticate employees, facilitate task management workflows, and provide administrative clearance. Data is never monetized or shared with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security Controls

Data is stored locally in hardened SQLite storage using Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and protected by zero-trust rate limiters and Content Security Policies. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

5. Inquiries & Administration

For inquiries regarding internal access permissions or data correction, contact your designated TetraSync executive administrator.
